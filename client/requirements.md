## Notes
- The site simulates a tech blog with a minimalist design.
- The `FakeOverlay` component intentionally blocks all user interaction on the tutorial pages to simulate a validation process that never completes (60-120s delay, then attempt to close or redirect).
- Static images are used from Unsplash as requested.
